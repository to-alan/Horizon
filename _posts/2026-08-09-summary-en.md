### [Harvey LAB Open-Sources a Benchmark for Legal AI Agents](https://github.com/harveyai/harvey-labs) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Harvey AI has released Harvey LAB, an MIT-licensed open-source benchmark for evaluating AI agents on realistic legal-work tasks. The project includes 1,671 tasks spanning more than 24 legal practice areas and contracting, with each task containing agent instructions, documents, and rubrics. An execution harness runs agents against the tasks and produces evaluations, reports, and comparison dashboards; the documentation describes all-pass rubric scoring and LLM-judge behavior. Harvey says the benchmark and harness are ongoing projects that will be expanded and refined, while the supplied material does not provide validation results or evidence of adoption.

rss · GitHub Trending - Daily · Aug 9, 14:28

**「Background」** AI-agent benchmarks provide standardized tasks and scoring methods for comparing how well systems perform practical work, rather than relying only on general language-model tests. Harvey LAB applies this approach to legal work through a task dataset containing instructions, documents, and rubrics, plus an execution harness that runs and evaluates agents against those tasks. The project is intended to help organizations identify which parts of legal workflows agents can fully support, partially assist with, or not yet handle reliably.

**「Practical Impact」** Researchers and developers can use a shared, extensible task set and execution harness to compare legal-agent systems under more realistic workflows than generic language-model tests.
