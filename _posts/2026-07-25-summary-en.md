## [Two Chinese Mathematicians Win 2026 Fields Medal](https://t.me/zaihuapd/42748) ⭐️ 10.0/10

The International Mathematical Union awarded the 2026 Fields Medal to Deng Yu and John Pardon, marking the first time mathematicians of Chinese nationality have received the prize. Deng was recognized for work on partial differential equations, including the rigorous derivation of the Boltzmann equation from hard-sphere dynamics, and Pardon for advances in symplectic geometry, including new virtual fundamental cycle methods. This achievement signals a major shift in the global recognition of Chinese mathematics, as the Fields Medal is the highest honor in the field. Their breakthroughs in PDEs and symplectic geometry will influence future research in kinetic theory and mirror symmetry. The Fields Medal is awarded every four years to mathematicians under 40. Deng Yu's work includes a long-time derivation of the Boltzmann equation from hard-sphere dynamics, extending Lanford's theorem, while John Pardon contributed new virtual fundamental cycle techniques and studied Fukaya categories of certain manifolds.

**Background**: The Fields Medal, often considered the Nobel Prize of mathematics, recognizes outstanding mathematical achievements by young researchers. The Boltzmann equation describes the statistical behavior of a gas, and its rigorous derivation from microscopic dynamics is a long-standing problem. Symplectic geometry is a branch of differential geometry that studies structures arising from physics, and virtual fundamental cycles and Fukaya categories are advanced tools for counting curves and understanding mirror symmetry.

## [Microsoft SkillOpt: Train Agent Skills Like Neural Networks](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillOpt) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Microsoft released SkillOpt v0.2.0, a text-space optimizer that trains reusable natural-language skills for frozen LLM agents through trajectory-driven edits and validation-gated updates, without modifying model weights. The new version includes the SkillOpt-Sleep nightly offline self-evolution engine and integration shells for Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, and Devin. SkillOpt bridges the gap between neural network training and skill learning for LLM agents, enabling performance improvements without fine-tuning or changing model weights. This approach allows agents to evolve skills in a deployable, reusable format (best_skill.md), which could significantly reduce the cost and complexity of adapting LLM agents to new tasks. SkillOpt treats a compact natural-language skill document as the trainable state of a frozen LLM agent, using rollouts, reflection, bounded edits, and held-out validation gates to optimize it. The optimizer introduces concepts like epochs, batch sizes, learning rates, and validation in text space.

rss · GitHub Trending - Python Daily · Jul 24, 01:47

**Background**: Large language model (LLM) agents often struggle to perform well on specific tasks without fine-tuning, which is expensive and risky. SkillOpt addresses this by learning reusable natural-language skills—essentially prompt strategies—that can be injected into a frozen agent to improve its performance. The process is inspired by neural network training: the skill document is iteratively edited based on task trajectories and validated against a held-out set, ensuring only beneficial changes are retained.
