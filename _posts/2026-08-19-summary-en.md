### [Moderna and Merck Report Phase 3 mRNA Cancer Vaccine Success](https://www.ithome.com/0/991/854.htm) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Moderna and Merck Sharp &amp; Dohme announced that their individualized mRNA melanoma vaccine intismeran autogene, formerly mRNA-4157/V940, met the primary endpoints in the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial when combined with Keytruda. The trial enrolled 1,137 high-risk melanoma patients with fully resected stage IIB to IV disease and compared Keytruda alone with Keytruda plus up to 9 doses of the personalized vaccine. The companies have disclosed only top-line Phase 3 results so far, and intismeran autogene remains investigational pending full data release and regulatory review.

### [Dark Cavity Boosts Superconductivity](https://www.ithome.com/0/991/862.htm) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A research team led by the University of Science and Technology of China, with collaborators from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and MIT, reported in Nature on August 19 that a &quot;dark cavity&quot; can enhance superconductivity through vacuum fluctuations. The team used a terahertz split-ring resonator cavity to embed niobium diselenide \(NbSe2\) and observed that the superconducting critical temperature rose by up to 5.4% in a six-layer device. They also reported higher critical current and critical magnetic field near the superconducting transition, and said this is the first experimental observation of vacuum-fluctuation-driven enhancement of superconductivity. The authors attribute the effect to coupling between the superconductor and the cavity&\#x27;s virtual photons, with the strongest enhancement appearing when the cavity mode matches the material&\#x27;s low-energy fluctuations.

rss · IT之家 · Aug 19, 15:26

**「Background」** In quantum electrodynamics, vacuum fluctuations are temporary field fluctuations that remain even when no external light is present. A dark cavity is a resonant structure designed to reshape the local electromagnetic environment without injecting photons, which can make those fluctuations more influential for nearby materials.

**「Impact」** The result gives superconductivity researchers a new non-contact way to tune material behavior through cavity engineering, which could matter for future quantum materials and device designs.

### [SIREN Symmetry and Weight-Space Gaps](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vswdnf/how_much_of_the_weightspace_perception_gap_is/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A Reddit post describes a large empirical study on SIREN-style implicit neural representations that asks whether symmetry, rather than something deeper, explains why weight-space models work better on networks trained from a shared initialization than on independently fitted ones. The author says they proved generic one-hidden-layer identifiability modulo the relevant symmetry group, then extended the analysis to depth two with cross-layer invariants built from the second-layer Gram matrix. In experiments across roughly 1.8 million fitted INRs on MNIST, FashionMNIST, and CIFAR-10, randomizing only the exact symmetry group while keeping each network’s function fixed removed 79.1 of the 80.4 MNIST accuracy points in the shared-init versus random-init gap. The post also reports that a function-space approach still outperformed the best weight-space reader in a FLOPs-matched comparison, reaching 95.3% at 1.6 MFLOPs versus 64.4% at 5.5 MFLOPs.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/ITheClixs · Aug 19, 19:24

**「Background」** SIRENs are implicit neural representations that use sine activations, so multiple parameter settings can represent the same function through symmetries such as neuron permutations and sign or phase transformations. Weight-space learning tries to predict properties of a network directly from its parameters, which makes it sensitive to whether two models are aligned by shared initialization or have been fitted independently.

**「Impact」** If the reported results hold up, they suggest that symmetry handling is a major requirement for weight-space models on SIREN-like networks, and that querying the network function can remain more efficient than working in parameter space even when a complete invariant is available.
