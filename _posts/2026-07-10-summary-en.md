## [Ant LingBot open-sources LingBot-Video, first MoE embodied video foundation model](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/07/446458.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Ant LingBot open-sourced LingBot-Video, the world's first MoE-based embodied video generation foundation model, on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license. The model has 30B total parameters but activates only 3B per inference, achieving approximately 3x the inference efficiency of a comparable dense model. This marks a milestone in embodied AI by combining MoE with video generation, enabling efficient and scalable video synthesis for robot action prediction, simulation data generation, and world model research. The open-source release lowers barriers for the community to advance embodied intelligence. LingBot-Video uses a DiT+MoE architecture, a data engine with 70,000 hours of embodied data covering dexterous manipulation, robot mobility, and first-person interaction, and a multi-dimensional RL reward system focusing on physical plausibility and task completion. On the RBench benchmark for robot-oriented video generation, it achieved a total score of 0.620, surpassing models like Wan2.6, Seedance1.5 Pro, and Cosmos3 Super.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 9, 04:30

**Background**: MoE (Mixture of Experts) is a neural network architecture that divides the model into multiple 'experts' and activates only a subset per input, improving efficiency without sacrificing capacity. DiT (Diffusion Transformer) is a diffusion model using Vision Transformer as its backbone, widely adopted for high-quality image and video generation. Embodied AI focuses on agents that can perceive and act in physical environments, with video generation models serving as world simulators for planning and training.

This achievement demonstrates DJI's advanced VTOL capabilities in extreme high-altitude environments, paving the way for drone logistics and atmospheric research in challenging terrains. It also highlights the potential of eVTOL drones for cargo delivery and scientific data collection. The EV50 is a composite-wing VTOL drone that can take off and land vertically and switch to fixed-wing cruise. During the mission, it completed 32 sorties, climbed 3,730 meters continuously, and still had 30% battery remaining upon return, carrying ozone-measuring equipment for Peking University.

**Background**: VTOL (Vertical Take-Off and Landing) drones combine the benefits of multi-rotor and fixed-wing designs, allowing vertical operations with efficient long-range cruise. Composite-wing UAVs, also known as hybrid drones, use separate lift and propulsion systems for vertical and forward flight, enabling high endurance and flexibility. This mission was part of the 'Peak Mission' Everest scientific expedition.
